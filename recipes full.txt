Default Grilled Chicken (Batch-Prep Edition)
A high-yield, lean batch-cooking method engineered for meal prep. Draining the released liquid midway prevents the meat from boiling in its own juices and keeps it tender rather than rubbery.

Ingredients & Gear
Boneless Chicken: 2 kg (breast or thigh), cut into even strips

Plain Yogurt (Dahi): 1/2 standard cup (~120–125 g)

Pre-mixed Spice Blend: 1 packet (~45–50 g) Chicken Tikka or Tandoori masala (Shan, National, etc.)

Optional flavor lifter: 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste and 1–2 tbsp lemon juice (adds brightness to pre-packaged masala)

Equipment: Large rimmed baking sheet/casserole tray, cutting board, tongs, and freezer-safe ziplock bags

Instructions
Prep the Meat

Pat the chicken strips dry with paper towels to avoid excess moisture. Slice into uniform strips (~1 inch thick) so they cook at the identical rate.

Marinade

In a large bowl, whisk together the yogurt and chosen spice blend (plus ginger-garlic paste/lemon juice if using). Toss the chicken strips in the marinade until fully coated.

(Quick cook: use immediately. Best flavor: let sit in the fridge for 30–60 minutes).

First Bake (The Render Phase)

Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F).

Spread the chicken strips across a high-rimmed baking tray in a single, even layer.

Bake for 35–40 minutes. The chicken will release a significant amount of liquid and juices.

Drain & Reset

Carefully pull the tray from the oven. Using tongs or holding a lid/spatula against the edge, pour off the pooled liquid from the corner into a heat-safe bowl or drain it carefully.

Finish & Dry (The Sear Phase)

Return the tray to the oven for an additional 10–15 minutes until the surface is no longer wet and begins to look matte with light browning around the edges.

Pro-move: Switch the oven to Broil / High Grill for the final 3–4 minutes to catch authentic charred tandoori edges without drying out the interior.

Cool & Portion

Let the chicken rest at room temperature for 15–20 minutes before packing. Divide into single-meal portions (e.g., 150–200 g per bag) in ziplock bags, press out excess air, and freeze.

Chef’s Notes
Don't throw out the chicken liquid entirely: That drained liquid is seasoned chicken stock with melted spices. Instead of trashing it, reduce it in a small saucepan for 5 minutes with a splash of water to make a quick sauce for rice, or pour a spoonful back into your frozen bags when reheating.

Thawing tip: Reheat straight from the freezer on a dry, medium-hot skillet with a lid on for 3–4 minutes; the trapped steam rehydrates the yogurt marinade instantly.



Miscellaneous Rice (TM)
A foolproof, adaptable savory rice base designed to pair with any protein or curry. The gentle aromatics and stock cube give it body, while the final reduction and low-steam stage ensure separate, fluffy grains without getting mushy.

Ingredients
Basmati Rice: 1 standard measuring cup

Cooking Oil: 2 tbsp (neutral cooking oil or ghee)

Onion: ½ small or ¼ large onion, thinly sliced

Liquid Base: 2 ¼ cups water

Flavor Core:

1 level tsp fine salt

¼ tsp freshly cracked black pepper

½ chicken bouillon/stock cube (crumbled)

Optional Enhancements (The "Choose Your Own Adventure" Additions):

Garlic: 1–2 cloves minced or sliced, or ½ tsp garlic paste/powder

Heat: ½ tsp red chilli flakes / crushed red pepper (for dry heat)

Aromatics: 1 bay leaf, 2 green cardamom pods, or a pinch of cumin seeds (zeera) tossed in with the onions

Step-by-Step Instructions
Rinse & Soak the Rice

Measure 1 cup of basmati rice into a bowl.

Rinse thoroughly with cold tap water, gently agitating the grains with your fingers. Drain and repeat 3–4 times until the water runs clear (this removes excess starch so the rice doesn't clump).

Fill the bowl with fresh water and let the rice soak while you prep the stock.

Fry the Aromatics

Place a medium pot or saucepan over medium heat and add 2 tbsp of cooking oil.

Add the sliced onions (and whole spices/fresh garlic if using).

Fry until the onions are just barely golden brown around the edges—keep an eye on them so they don't darken and turn bitter.

Build the Broth

Pour in 2 ¼ cups of water.

Add 1 level tsp of salt, black pepper, and the crumbled ½ chicken stock cube (plus red chilli flakes/garlic powder if using).

Stir gently to dissolve the cube and bring the broth up to a rolling boil over high heat.

Add Rice & Simmer

Thoroughly drain the soaked rice in a strainer.

Once the pot is boiling vigorously, slide the drained rice into the water.

Lower the heat to medium-low, cover tightly with a lid, and let it simmer. Check after 5–7 minutes: the grains will absorb liquid and expand significantly.

Reduce Surface Moisture

Once the rice has swelled up to fill the pot but you still see bubbling liquid when gently parting the grains with a spoon, remove the lid.

Turn the heat up to medium for about 1 to 2 minutes to cook off the excess standing water from the bottom and sides.

The Steam Finish (Dum)

As soon as visible surface water recedes and you see small steam holes ("craters") opening across the top of the rice bed, turn the heat to the lowest possible setting.

Place the lid back on (wrap the underside of the lid in a clean kitchen towel for extra steam capture if desired).

Let it rest on lowest heat for 5–7 minutes, or simply turn the flame off entirely and leave it covered for 10 minutes.

Fluff gently with a fork from the edges inward before serving.

Quick Fix Notes
Basmati soak rule: A 15–20 minute soak elongates the grains and prevents them from breaking during the boil.

Salt balance check: If using a full stock cube or high-sodium brand, knock the salt down to ½ tsp so the rice doesn't end up overly salty.



Green Chilli Rice
A vibrant, zesty basmati rice infused with fresh green chillies sautéed directly with aromatics, enriched with whisked plain yogurt, and steamed to fluffy perfection with savory chicken stock.

Ingredients
Basmati Rice: 1 standard measuring cup

Cooking Oil or Ghee: 2 tbsp

Onion: ½ small or ¼ large onion, thinly sliced

Fresh Green Chillies: 2 to 3 green chillies, slit lengthwise or sliced into coins

Plain Yogurt (Dahi): ¼ standard cup (~60 g), whisked until smooth

Liquid Base: 2 cups water (reduced from 2 ¼ cups to compensate for yogurt moisture)

Flavor Core:

1 level tsp fine salt

¼ tsp freshly cracked black pepper

½ chicken bouillon/stock cube (crumbled)

Optional Enhancements:

Garlic: 1–2 cloves sliced or ½ tsp garlic paste

Aromatics: 1 bay leaf, 2 green cardamom pods, or a pinch of cumin seeds (zeera)

Step-by-Step Instructions
Rinse & Soak the Rice

Measure 1 cup of basmati rice into a bowl. Rinse thoroughly 3–4 times until the water runs clear. Cover with fresh water and let soak for 15–20 minutes. Whisk ¼ cup plain yogurt in a small bowl until completely smooth.

Fry the Aromatics & Green Chillies

Place a medium pot over medium heat and add 2 tbsp of cooking oil or ghee.

Add the sliced onion and green chillies (plus whole spices/garlic if using).

Sauté for 3 to 4 minutes until the onions turn light golden brown and the green chillies blister, releasing their floral heat into the fat.

Stir in Yogurt & Build the Broth

Turn the heat down to low. Pour in the ¼ cup whisked yogurt and stir continuously for 1 minute until it incorporates smoothly with the aromatics and oil without curdling.

Pour in 2 cups of water. Add 1 level tsp of salt, black pepper, and the crumbled ½ chicken stock cube.

Raise heat to high and bring the broth up to a rolling boil.

Add Rice & Simmer

Thoroughly drain the soaked rice. Once the broth is boiling vigorously, slide the drained rice into the pot.

Lower the heat to medium-low, cover tightly with a lid, and let it simmer for 5–7 minutes until the rice expands and absorbs most of the liquid.

Reduce Surface Moisture

Remove the lid. Turn heat up to medium for 1 to 2 minutes to cook off standing surface moisture until small steam vents ("craters") open on the rice surface.

The Steam Finish (Dum)

Drop the heat to the lowest possible setting.

Place the lid back on (optionally wrapped in a clean kitchen towel to catch drips).

Let it steam on lowest flame for 5–7 minutes, or turn the flame off entirely and leave covered for 10 minutes.

Fluff gently with a fork from the edges inward before serving.

Chef’s Notes
Preventing yogurt split: Whisking the yogurt smooth before adding and dropping the burner to low heat for the first 60 seconds of stirring ensures the dairy blends into a silky gravy base without separating.

Balanced moisture: Basmati rice needs 2 ¼ cups total liquid per cup of rice; the ¼ cup yogurt provides both rich fat and liquid, meaning 2 cups of water gives you textbook separate grains with zero sogginess.

Spice level control: Slit the chillies lengthwise for a fragrant, medium warmth; slice them into thin coins if you want fiery, tongue-tingling green heat throughout every forkful.



Easy Cheesy Pasta (with Crispy Chicken Topper)
A creamy, comforting pink/blush sauce pasta that comes together fast using pantry staples and pre-made crispy chicken for texture contrast.

Ingredients
Pasta: 1 serving bowl dry pasta (~100–120 g dry weight; penne, rigatoni, or fusilli work best)

Water & Salt: Pot of water + 1 to 2 tsp salt for boiling

Butter: 1 tbsp

Red Sauce Base: 3 to 4 tbsp prepared tomato sauce (pizza sauce, marinara, or tomato mascarpone sauce)

Cream: 1 standard pack dairy cooking cream or heavy cream (~200 ml)

Cheese: 1 to 2 generous handfuls shredded cheese (cheddar, mozzarella, or a pizza blend)

Aromatics & Spices:

1–2 cloves garlic (minced/grated) or ½ tsp garlic powder

¼ tsp freshly cracked black pepper

½ tsp red chilli flakes (crushed chillies)

½ tsp dried oregano or mixed Italian herbs

Protein Topper: 3 to 4 frozen chicken tenders, strips, or crispy fillets

Instructions
Fire Up the Chicken

Pop the frozen chicken tenders into the air fryer or preheated oven according to package instructions (typically 10–12 minutes at 200°C in an air fryer). Get them extra crispy.

Start the Pasta

Bring a medium pot of water to a rolling boil and stir in 1 to 2 tsp of salt.

Drop in your measured dry pasta and cook until al dente (check the package for exact minutes; usually 8–10 minutes).

Build the Sauce Base

While the pasta cooks, place a wide skillet or frying pan over low heat.

Melt 1 tbsp of butter, then stir in 3 to 4 tbsp of your tomato/pizza sauce.

Add the garlic, black pepper, crushed chillies, and herbs. Let the aromatics cook gently in the butter and sauce for 1 to 2 minutes until fragrant.

Add Cream & Cheese

Pour the entire pack of cream into the pan, stirring to blend it with the red sauce into a rich pink base.

Turn the heat off (or down to dead low) and scatter your shredded cheese over the surface. Turning the heat down prevents the cream and cheese from splitting into oil.

Emulsify with Pasta Water

Just before draining the cooked pasta, scoop out ½ cup of starchy boiling pasta water.

Pour the hot pasta water directly into the pan over the cheese.

Stir vigorously with a spatula or whisk—the residual heat and starch will smoothly melt and emulsify the cheese into a glossy, velvety sauce.

Toss & Top

Drain the rest of the pasta and immediately dump the hot noodles into the sauce pan. Toss well so the sauce clings to every groove.

Slice the hot, crispy chicken tenders into bite-sized strips and arrange them right over the top of the bowl. Serve hot.

Quick Fix Tips
Sauce thickness: If the sauce feels slightly thin when the pasta first hits it, turn the flame to low for 30–60 seconds while tossing; the pasta starch will thicken it instantly. If it tightens up too much, splash in another tablespoon of leftover pasta water.

Keep the chicken crunchy: Placing the sliced chicken directly on top rather than stirring it into the sauce keeps the breading crisp against the creamy pasta.



Easy French Bread Pizza (TM)
A fast, customizable flatbread-style pizza on crusty bread. The bread gives a crunchy bottom with a soft crumb, while the combination of spicy kebab/chicken, melting cheese, and a light mayo drizzle creates that classic bakery-style pizza flavor.

Ingredients
Bread Base: 1 footlong French roll, crusty baguette, or hoagie roll (split in half lengthwise)

Sauce: 3 to 4 tbsp tomato-based sauce (pizza sauce, marinara, or tomato-mascarpone sauce)

Vegetables:

¼ small onion, finely diced or thinly sliced

¼ bell pepper (capsicum), cut into small cubes or thin strips

Protein:

Option A: 1 cup diced Default Grilled Chicken

Option B: 2 frozen seekh kebabs (cooked per box directions, then sliced into coins)

Cheese: 1 to 1 ½ cups shredded cheese (mozzarella, mild cheddar, or a blend)

Finishing Drizzle: 1 to 2 tbsp mayonnaise (piped or zigzagged from a squeeze bottle)

Seasoning (To Taste):

Dried oregano

Red chilli flakes (crushed chillies)

Freshly ground black pepper

Instructions
Prep the Bread & Heat the Oven

Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).

Split the roll lengthwise and place both halves cut-side up on a baking sheet lined with foil or baking paper.

Anti-sogginess trick: If your bread crumb is very soft, pop the bare bread into the oven for 2 minutes first to crisp the surface slightly before saucing.

Sauce & Veggie Layer

Spread an even layer of tomato sauce over both cut sides, taking it close to the crust edges.

Scatter the chopped onion and bell pepper evenly across the sauce base.

Protein & Cheese

Lay down your protein of choice:

If using Default Grilled Chicken, shred or dice it and distribute evenly.

If using Seekh Kebabs, heat them in a skillet, air fryer, or microwave just enough to thaw/cook through, slice into thin rounds, and arrange across the bread.

Blanket everything generously with shredded cheese, covering the edges of the bread to keep them from scorching.

Sauce Drizzle & Seasoning

Drizzle a thin zigzag of mayonnaise over the cheese.

Dust with a pinch of dried oregano, crushed chillies, and cracked black pepper.

Bake & Melt

Slide the tray into the oven at 180°C.

Bake for 5 to 8 minutes until the bread is crunchy and the cheese is fully melted.

Switch the oven to Broil / Top Grill for the final 1 to 2 minutes so the cheese bubbles and develops blistered golden spots, and the mayo slightly chars.

Slice & Serve

Transfer to a cutting board, let rest for 1 minute so the cheese settles, then slice each half into 3 or 4 shareable planks with a bread knife.



Quick Seekh Kabab Karahi (TM)
A fast, savory dhaba-style karahi that turns frozen or leftover kebabs into a rich, tomato-forward curry in under 15 minutes.

Ingredients
Cooking Fat: 3 to 4 tbsp oil, ghee, or butter

Seekh Kebabs: 4 pre-cooked or frozen seekh kebabs (beef or chicken), cut into thirds (~12 bite-sized pieces)

Onion: ½ small onion, finely sliced or chopped

Tomato Base: ½ can diced or crushed tomatoes (~200 g / ~7 oz)

Spices & Seasoning:

⅓ tsp red chilli powder or cayenne pepper

¼ tsp fine salt (adjust to taste, since packaged kebabs already contain salt)

Flavor Finishers (Optional but recommended):

1 tsp ginger-garlic paste (added with the onions)

½ tsp cumin powder (zeera) or garam masala

1–2 fresh green chillies, slit lengthwise

Fresh coriander and ginger matchsticks (julienned) for garnish

Instructions
Brown the Aromatics

Heat 3 to 4 tbsp of oil or butter in a wok (karahi) or deep skillet over medium heat.

Add the sliced onion (and ginger-garlic paste, if using).

Sauté for 3 to 5 minutes until the onion edges just turn translucent and light golden brown.

Sear the Kebabs

Toss in the kebab pieces.

Fry them alongside the onions for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring gently so they pick up light color and crisp edges without breaking apart.

Add Tomatoes & Seasoning

Pour in the ½ can of crushed tomatoes.

Sprinkle in the ⅓ tsp red chilli powder and ¼ tsp salt (plus cumin/garam masala if using).

Stir gently to combine, coating the kebabs in the sauce.

Simmer & Bhunai (Caramelize)

Lower the heat to medium-low and let the mixture simmer uncovered.

Cook for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring occasionally. You want the harsh acidity of the canned tomatoes to mellow and the sauce to thicken until it darkens slightly, turns glossy, and the oil starts to separate (rogaan) at the edges of the pan.

Finish & Serve

Drop in the slit green chillies during the last 2 minutes of cooking.

Take off the heat, top with fresh coriander and ginger juliennes, and serve piping hot straight from the skillet with warm roti, naan, or paratha.



Easy Chicken Handi (Creamy Dhaba Style)
A silky, mildly spiced stovetop white handi that skips complicated gravy bases. The chicken sears directly with the aromatics before simmering down in rich dairy, finished with a melted cheese option for extra velvet.

Ingredients
Boneless Chicken Breast: ~500 g (½ kg), cut into 1-inch bite-sized cubes

Cooking Fat: 2 to 3 tbsp oil, ghee, or butter

Aromatics:

½ small onion, finely diced

2 to 3 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 tsp garlic paste)

Spices & Seasoning:

½ tsp fine salt (or to taste)

¼ tsp crushed red chilli flakes

¼ tsp freshly cracked black pepper (to finish)

Dairy & Texture:

½ container cooking cream or heavy dairy cream (~100–125 ml)

Optional: 1 small handful shredded mozzarella or cheddar cheese (~30–40 g)

Garnish / Optional Add-ins:

A pinch of dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi, crushed between your palms)

Freshly chopped coriander or julienned ginger

Instructions
Sauté the Aromatics

Heat 2 to 3 tbsp of oil or butter in a handi, wok, or wide skillet over medium heat.

Add the finely diced onion and minced garlic.

Sauté for 3 to 4 minutes until the onions turn translucent and soft, without letting the garlic burn.

Sear the Chicken

Toss the cubed chicken into the pan.

Fry on medium-high heat for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally.

The chicken will turn opaque white, pick up faint golden spots on the edges, and begin releasing its natural juices into the pan.

Season & Cream Base

Turn the heat down to medium-low.

Stir in ½ tsp salt and ¼ tsp crushed chilli flakes.

Pour in the ½ container of cream. Stir thoroughly to emulsify the cream with the chicken juices and rendered fat.

Simmer to Thickness

Let the handi simmer gently over low heat for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally so the dairy doesn't catch on the bottom.

Cook until the chicken is fully tender, the sauce turns creamy and homogeneous, and you see small beads of oil separating around the perimeter.

Taste check: Sample the gravy here—adjust salt or chilli flakes if needed.

Cheese Melt & Finish

Turn the heat completely off.

Optional step: Scatter the shredded cheese over the surface, pop the lid on for 1 minute to let it melt in the residual steam, then gently fold it through the gravy until velvety smooth.

Dust with fresh black pepper and rubbed kasuri methi. Garnish with freshly chopped coriander and julienned ginger before serving.

Quick Fix Tips
Prevent cream split: Keep the heat on low when adding cream, and never let it boil violently.

Juiciness check: Cubed breast cooks fast—keep total cook time around 12–15 minutes max so the cubes stay tender rather than chalky.



Crispy & Tangy Grilled Chicken Sandwich
A classic deli-style stack built to balance the spiced chicken with cold crunch, acidity, and a double-sauce barrier to keep the bread crisp.

Ingredients
Bread: 2 thick slices of your favorite sandwich bread (sourdough, brioche, or classic sandwich white)

Chicken: 1 generous cup chopped cooked chicken (your Default Grilled Chicken works perfectly here)

Sauces:

1 ½ to 2 tbsp standard mayonnaise (divided)

1 tbsp sriracha mayo (or regular mayo mixed with a splash of sriracha/hot sauce)

Crunch & Acid:

1 small handful shredded iceberg or romaine lettuce

3 to 4 dill or sandwich pickle slices (crinkle-cut or bread & butter)

Optional upgrade: 1 tsp butter for pan-toasting the bread

Instructions
Toast the Bread (Recommended)

Lightly butter the outer sides of both bread slices.

Toast on a hot skillet until golden brown and crisp, or run them through a regular toaster. Let them sit on a wire rack or cutting board for 60 seconds so trapped steam doesn’t make the bottoms soggy.

The Moisture Barrier (Bottom Layer)

Lay the bottom slice flat.

Spread an even layer of regular mayonnaise across the crumb.

Scatter the shredded lettuce directly on top of the mayo. (The mayo acts as a glue, and the lettuce creates a barrier so the chicken juices don’t soak into the bread.)

Protein & Spice

Pile the warm or cold chopped grilled chicken evenly over the bed of lettuce.

Drizzle or spread the sriracha mayo directly across the chicken for heat and creaminess.

Pickles & Top Seal

Layer 3 to 4 pickle slices across the sauced chicken to provide sharp acidity to cut through the mayo.

Smear a thin dab of regular mayo over the top bread slice (to lock the pickles in place) and cap the sandwich.

Cut & Serve

Press down gently with your palm to compact the layers.

Slice diagonally with a sharp serrated knife and serve immediately.



Pizza Upgrade
The ultimate cheat code for pizza night—doctoring up a basic, inexpensive frozen or deli cheese pizza into a loaded, bakery-style pie with seasoned chicken or kebabs, peppers, an extra cheese blanket, and garlic-mayo drizzle.

Ingredients
Pizza Base: 1 plain frozen or deli cheese pizza (10–12 inches) (no extra sauce needed)

Protein: 1 cup chopped Default Grilled Chicken OR 2 cooked Seekh Kebabs (sliced into thin rounds)

Veggies (To Taste):

¼ small onion, thinly sliced or finely diced

¼ green bell pepper (capsicum), thinly sliced or cubed

Cheese Blanket:

½ to 1 cup additional shredded cheese (mozzarella, cheddar, or pizza blend)

Seasoning & Drizzle:

½ tsp garlic powder

½ to 1 tsp crushed red chilli flakes

1 to 2 tbsp mayonnaise (in a squeeze bottle or drizzled with a spoon)

Optional: Pinch of dried oregano

Instructions
Preheat the Oven

Crank your oven to 200°C (400°F).

Preheating a baking stone or sheet pan inside the oven ensures the bottom crust turns crunchy and never soggy.

The Base Setup

Unwrap your plain frozen or deli cheese pizza. Lay it directly on your prep board or baking sheet—no extra sauce needed, as the cheese and sauce foundation is already in place.

Layer the Toppings

Distribute your chopped grilled chicken or sliced seekh kebabs evenly across the surface.

Scatter the sliced onions and bell peppers over the meat.

Cheese Blanket & Seasoning

Top with your extra shredded cheese to lock the toppings in place and prevent the meat from drying out under high heat.

Dust evenly with garlic powder, crushed red chilli flakes, and dried oregano.

Zigzag a thin spiral or grid of mayonnaise over the top.

Bake to Golden

Slide onto the preheated baking sheet on the middle oven rack.

Bake for 9 to 12 minutes until the bottom crust is firm and crispy.

The Finish: Switch to Broil / High Grill for the final 1 to 2 minutes until the cheese is bubbling with toasted brown spots and the mayo slightly sizzles.

Slice & Serve

Let it rest on a cutting board for 2 minutes (so the melted cheese doesn't slide off when cut), slice into 6 or 8 wedges, and serve hot.

Chef’s Notes
Lock toppings with cheese: Putting the extra cheese over the meat and vegetables traps steam, keeping the pre-cooked chicken or kebabs juicy rather than dry and tough.

Preheated tray effect: Sliding the pizza onto a baking sheet that has been heating inside the oven instantly sets the bottom crust, eliminating soggy centers.



Easy Pizza
A fast, customizable personal pizza built from a bare par-baked pizza crust. Layered with seasoned pizza sauce, a baseline cheese scatter, high-yield protein, crisp peppers and onions, an oven-melted cheese blanket, and a tangy mayo drizzle.

Ingredients
Pizza Base: 1 bare par-baked pizza crust / base (10–12 inches)

Sauce: 3 to 4 tbsp pizza sauce or prepared tomato sauce (marinara)

Shredded Cheese: 1 to 1 ½ cups shredded cheese (mozzarella, cheddar, or pizza blend), divided: ~¼ cup for baseline scatter, remainder for top blanket

Protein: 1 cup chopped Default Grilled Chicken OR 2 cooked Seekh Kebabs (sliced into thin rounds)

Veggies (To Taste):

¼ small onion, thinly sliced or finely diced

¼ green bell pepper (capsicum), thinly sliced or cubed

Seasoning & Drizzle:

½ tsp garlic powder

½ to 1 tsp crushed red chilli flakes

1 to 2 tbsp mayonnaise (in a squeeze bottle or drizzled with a spoon)

Optional: Pinch of dried oregano

Instructions
Preheat the Oven

Crank your oven to 200°C (400°F).

Preheat a baking sheet or pizza stone inside the oven to ensure a crisp, sturdy bottom crust.

Sauce & Base Foundation

Place the bare crust on a prep tray or cutting board.

Spread 3 to 4 tbsp of tomato sauce evenly, leaving a ½-inch border around the edge for the crust.

Scatter a light baseline layer of shredded cheese (~¼ cup) directly over the sauce. (This anchors the toppings and keeps the dough from getting soggy).

Layer the Toppings

Distribute your chopped grilled chicken or sliced seekh kebabs evenly across the surface.

Scatter the sliced onions and bell peppers over the meat.

Cheese Blanket & Seasoning

Top with the remaining shredded cheese to lock the toppings in place right up to the crust edge.

Dust evenly with garlic powder, crushed red chilli flakes, and dried oregano.

Zigzag a thin spiral or grid of mayonnaise over the top.

Bake to Golden

Slide onto the preheated baking sheet on the middle oven rack.

Bake for 8 to 10 minutes until the bottom crust is firm and crispy.

The Finish: Switch to Broil / High Grill for the final 1 to 2 minutes until the cheese is blistered with toasted golden spots and the mayo sizzles.

Slice & Serve

Let it rest on a cutting board for 2 minutes (so the molten cheese doesn't slide off when cut), slice into 6 or 8 wedges, and serve hot.

Chef’s Notes
Baseline cheese secret: Scattering a light layer of cheese before adding the toppings acts as culinary glue between sauce and meat, preventing the entire topping layer from sliding off on your first bite.

Baking on preheated metal: Bare par-baked crusts cook fast. Putting them on a sizzling-hot preheated baking sheet gives that authentic pizzeria crunch on the base within 8 minutes.
