1. Default Grilled Chicken (Batch-Prep Edition)
Ingredients
2 kg boneless chicken (breast or thigh), cut into uniform strips (~1 inch wide)

½ cup (~120–125 g) plain yogurt (dahi)

1 packet (~45–50 g) pre-mixed Chicken Tikka or Tandoori masala

Optional: 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste and 1–2 tbsp lemon juice

Step-by-Step Instructions
Preheat & Prep

Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Pat the sliced chicken strips dry with paper towels to prevent excess moisture build-up.

Mix Marinade

In a large bowl, whisk the yogurt with the masala packet (and optional ginger-garlic paste/lemon juice). Add the chicken strips and toss until thoroughly coated.

Bake Phase 1 (Juice Release)

Spread the chicken in an even, single layer on a high-rimmed baking sheet or roasting dish. Bake at 200°C for 35–40 minutes.

Drain the Pan

Carefully remove the tray from the oven. Using a spatula or tongs to hold back the meat, pour off the pooled liquid into a bowl or the sink. (Do not let the chicken dry out by attempting to boil this liquid off in the oven).

Bake Phase 2 (Dry & Char)

Return the drained tray to the oven for 10–15 minutes until the chicken stops appearing wet. Switch to high broil/grill for the final 2–3 minutes for lightly charred edges.

Cool & Portion

Let the meat cool for 15–20 minutes. Divide into freezer-safe ziplock bags (~150–200 g portions) and freeze.

Simultaneous Operations:

While the chicken is on its 35–40 minute first bake, prep and measure ingredients for side dishes (like rice or pasta) or clean prep bowls.

2. Miscellaneous Rice
Ingredients
1 cup basmati rice

2 tbsp cooking oil or ghee

½ small (or ¼ large) onion, thinly sliced

2 ¼ cups water

1 level tsp fine salt

¼ tsp black pepper

½ chicken stock cube, crumbled

Optional Add-ins: 1–2 cloves sliced garlic, ½ tsp crushed red pepper (or chilli flakes for dry heat), or whole cumin seeds / bay leaf

Step-by-Step Instructions
Start Aromatics

Heat 2 tbsp oil in a medium pot over medium heat. Add the sliced onion (and whole spices/fresh garlic if using). Sauté until the onions turn barely golden brown.

Build the Broth

Pour in 2 ¼ cups of water. Add 1 level tsp salt, black pepper, and crumbled ½ chicken stock cube (plus dried chillies/garlic powder if using). Increase the heat to high to bring to a rolling boil.

Simmer Covered

Drain the soaked rice completely. Once the pot is boiling vigorously, drop the rice in. Lower the heat to medium-low, cover with a tight-fitting lid, and let simmer for 5–7 minutes.

Evaporate Surface Liquid

Once the rice has swelled to take up most of the liquid but pooling is still visible when gently stirred, remove the lid. Raise heat to medium for ~2 minutes to drive off surface water.

Steam Finish (Dum)

When visible surface water is gone and small steam vents form, drop heat to the lowest possible setting. Cover tightly and let steam for 5–7 minutes (or turn flame off and let sit covered for 10 minutes). Fluff with a fork before serving.

Simultaneous Operations:

While the broth comes to a boil in Step 2: Wash the 1 cup of basmati rice in a separate bowl until the water runs clear, then submerge it in fresh water to soak until the broth boils.

3. Green Chilli Rice
Ingredients
1 cup basmati rice

2 tbsp cooking oil or ghee

½ small (or ¼ large) onion, thinly sliced

2–3 fresh green chillies, slit lengthwise or sliced

¼ cup (~60 g) plain yogurt (dahi), whisked smooth

2 cups water

1 level tsp fine salt

¼ tsp black pepper

½ chicken stock cube, crumbled

Optional Add-ins: 1–2 cloves sliced garlic, whole cumin seeds / bay leaf

Step-by-Step Instructions
Start Aromatics & Chillies

Heat 2 tbsp oil or ghee in a medium pot over medium heat. Add the sliced onion and green chillies (and whole spices/fresh garlic if using). Sauté until the onions turn barely golden brown and the chillies blister and turn fragrant.

Blend Yogurt & Build Broth

Lower the heat to low. Stir in ¼ cup whisked yogurt and cook for 1 minute until well blended into the aromatics. Pour in 2 cups of water. Add 1 level tsp salt, black pepper, and crumbled ½ chicken stock cube. Increase heat to high to bring to a rolling boil.

Simmer Covered

Drain the soaked rice completely. Once the pot is boiling vigorously, drop the rice in. Lower the heat to medium-low, cover with a tight-fitting lid, and let simmer for 5–7 minutes.

Evaporate Surface Liquid

Once the rice has swelled to take up most of the liquid, remove the lid. Raise heat to medium for ~2 minutes to drive off standing surface water until small steam vents open.

Steam Finish (Dum)

When surface water is gone, drop heat to the lowest possible setting. Cover tightly and let steam for 5–7 minutes (or turn flame off and let sit covered for 10 minutes). Fluff gently with a fork before serving.

Simultaneous Operations:

While the rice soaks for 15 minutes before cooking: Slice onions and chillies, whisk the yogurt, and crumble the stock cube.

4. Easy Cheasy Pasta with Crispy Chicken
Ingredients
1 standard bowl (~100–120 g) dry pasta (penne, fusilli, or rigatoni)

Water + 1–2 tsp salt (for boiling)

1 tbsp butter

3–4 tbsp prepared tomato-based sauce (pizza sauce, marinara, or tomato-mascarpone sauce)

1 pack (~200 ml) dairy cooking cream or heavy cream

1–2 generous handfuls shredded cheese (mozzarella, cheddar, or pizza mix)

Aromatics: 1–2 cloves minced garlic (or ½ tsp garlic powder), ¼ tsp black pepper, ½ tsp crushed red chillies, ½ tsp dried oregano

Topper: 3–4 frozen chicken tenders/cutlets

Step-by-Step Instructions
Start the Chicken

Cook the frozen chicken tenders in an air fryer (10–12 minutes at 200°C) or oven per package directions until crispy.

Cook Pasta

Bring a salted pot of water to a rolling boil. Drop the dry pasta in and cook until al dente (approx. 8–10 minutes).

Sauté the Red Base

Melt 1 tbsp butter in a separate frying pan over low heat. Add 3–4 tbsp tomato sauce, garlic, black pepper, crushed chillies, and oregano. Sauté gently on low for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.

Cream & Cheese Addition

Pour the entire pack of cream into the sauce pan, stirring to blend into a pink sauce. Turn off the heat (or set to lowest flame) and scatter the shredded cheese over the sauce.

Emulsify

Right before straining the pasta, ladle out ½ cup of starchy boiling pasta water. Pour it directly over the cheese in the skillet. Stir vigorously until the cheese melts into a smooth, glossy sauce.

Combine & Assemble

Drain the pasta and fold it directly into the sauce. Slice the hot crispy tenders into bite-sized pieces, scatter over the top of the pasta bowl, and serve.

Simultaneous Operations:

Put the chicken tenders in the air fryer at the exact same moment you put the water on the stove to boil for pasta; both finish simultaneously.

5. Easy French Bread Pizza
Ingredients
1 footlong French roll, baguette, or hoagie roll (crusty variety, split lengthwise)

3–4 tbsp tomato-based sauce (pizza sauce, marinara, or mascarpone sauce)

¼ small onion, finely diced

¼ bell pepper (capsicum), finely diced or sliced

Protein (choose one):

1 cup diced Default Grilled Chicken

2 pre-cooked/frozen Seekh Kebabs (heated through and sliced into rounds)

1 to 1 ½ cups shredded cheese (mozzarella or cheddar mix)

1–2 tbsp mayonnaise

Seasoning: Dried oregano, crushed chillies, and black pepper

Step-by-Step Instructions
Prep Base & Oven

Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Slice the roll in half lengthwise and place both cut-side up on a baking tray.

Sauce & Veggies

Spread an even layer of tomato sauce over both halves. Scatter the diced onion and bell pepper over the sauce.

Add Protein & Cheese

Evenly distribute your chopped grilled chicken or sliced seekh kebabs across the bread. Blanket everything generously with shredded cheese right to the crust edges.

Drizzle & Season

Drizzle a zigzag of mayonnaise over the top of the cheese. Dust with oregano, crushed chillies, and black pepper.

Bake & Broil

Bake at 180°C for 3–5 minutes until the cheese melts and the bread crisps. Switch to top broil/grill for 1–2 minutes until the cheese bubbles and develops golden spots. Slice and serve.

Simultaneous Operations:

Dice the onions, peppers, and pre-cook/warm the kebabs while the oven is preheating to 180°C.

6. Quick Seekh Kabab Karahi
Ingredients
3–4 tbsp cooking oil, ghee, or butter

½ small onion, thinly sliced

4 seekh kebabs (beef or chicken), cut into thirds (~12 pieces total)

½ can (~200 g) crushed or diced tomatoes

⅓ tsp red chilli powder or cayenne pepper

¼ tsp fine salt (adjust to taste)

Optional Finishers: 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste, ½ tsp cumin/garam masala, 1 slit green chilli, julienned ginger, fresh coriander

Step-by-Step Instructions
Sauté Aromatics

Heat 3–4 tbsp oil or butter in a skillet or karahi over medium heat. Add the sliced onion (and ginger-garlic paste if using) and cook until translucent and just turning light golden.

Brown Kebabs

Add the kebab pieces to the pan. Sauté alongside the onions for 2–3 minutes until lightly browned on the edges.

Tomato & Spice Simmer

Pour in ½ can of tomatoes. Add ⅓ tsp red chilli powder and ¼ tsp salt (plus cumin/garam masala if using). Stir gently to avoid breaking up the kebab pieces.

Bhunai (Caramelize & Reduce)

Simmer uncovered over medium-low heat for 6–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes reduce, darken slightly, and oil starts separating from the sauce.

Garnish & Serve

Drop in the slit green chilli for the final 2 minutes. Remove from heat, top with ginger juliennes and fresh coriander, and serve straight from the skillet.

Simultaneous Operations:

Slice the kebabs into thirds while the sliced onions are softening in the oil in Step 1.

7. Easy Chicken Handi (Creamy Dhaba Style)
Ingredients
500 g (½ kg) boneless chicken breast, cut into 1-inch cubes

2–3 tbsp oil, ghee, or butter

½ small onion, finely diced

2–3 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 tsp garlic paste)

½ tsp fine salt

¼ tsp crushed chilli flakes

½ container (~100–125 ml) heavy dairy cooking cream

Optional: 1 small handful shredded mozzarella or cheddar (~30–40 g)

Finishing: Freshly cracked black pepper, pinch of rubbed kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), fresh chopped coriander or julienned ginger for garnish

Step-by-Step Instructions
Fry Aromatics

Heat 2–3 tbsp oil or butter in a deep pan over medium heat. Add the diced onion and minced garlic. Sauté for 3–4 minutes until soft and translucent without scorching the garlic.

Sear Chicken

Add the cubed chicken breast. Sauté on medium-high for 5–7 minutes until the chicken turns opaque, catches slight golden color, and releases its natural juices.

Season & Cream

Turn the heat down to low/medium-low. Add ½ tsp salt and ¼ tsp crushed chillies. Pour in the ½ container of cream and stir to combine with the pan juices.

Simmer

Simmer gently on low heat for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the gravy turns creamy and homogeneous and oil begins separating at the perimeter. Check seasoning for salt and heat.

Cheese Melt & Finish

Turn the stove off. Scatter shredded cheese over the surface (optional), cover for 1 minute to melt, then fold through the gravy. Dust with black pepper, crushed kasuri methi, and garnish with fresh coriander and julienned ginger before serving.

Simultaneous Operations:

Cube the chicken breast while the onion and garlic are sweating in Step 1.

8. Tangy Grilled Chicken Sandwich
Ingredients
2 slices sandwich bread (sourdough, brioche, or white)

1 generous cup cooked Default Grilled Chicken, chopped

1 ½ to 2 tbsp mayonnaise (divided)

1 tbsp sriracha mayo

1 small handful shredded iceberg or romaine lettuce

3–4 dill or sandwich pickle slices

Optional: 1 tsp butter for pan-toasting bread

Step-by-Step Instructions
Toast Bread

Lightly butter the outside of both bread slices and toast on a skillet (or in a toaster) until golden and crisp. Place on a board for 1 minute to vent steam.

Bottom Layer (Moisture Barrier)

Spread half the mayonnaise over the bottom bread slice. Cover with shredded lettuce.

Meat & Spiced Sauce

Pile the chopped grilled chicken evenly over the lettuce. Drizzle the sriracha mayo directly across the chicken.

Acid & Top Seal

Layer the pickle slices over the chicken. Spread the remaining mayo on the top slice of bread and place it on the sandwich.

Press & Slice

Gently press the sandwich down with your palm, slice diagonally with a serrated knife, and serve.

Simultaneous Operations:

Chop cold or reheated grilled chicken while the bread is toasting in the skillet.

9. Pizza Upgrade
Ingredients
1 pre-baked plain frozen cheese pizza (10–12 inches)

Protein: 1 cup chopped Default Grilled Chicken or 2 cooked Seekh Kebabs (sliced into rounds)

Veggies: ¼ small onion (thinly sliced) and ¼ bell pepper (thinly sliced or diced)

Cheese Blanket: ½ to 1 cup additional shredded cheese (mozzarella or cheddar)

Seasoning & Drizzle: ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ to 1 tsp crushed red chillies, pinch of dried oregano, 1–2 tbsp mayonnaise

Step-by-Step Instructions
Preheat Oven

Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Place a baking sheet inside to heat up if you want a crispier base.

Set the Foundation

Unwrap the plain frozen or deli cheese pizza and place directly on a prep tray (no sauce needed).

Toppings & Cheese

Distribute the chopped chicken or sliced kebabs evenly over the pizza. Scatter the sliced onions and bell peppers. Blanket the toppings with the extra shredded cheese.

Season & Drizzle

Dust the top with garlic powder, crushed chillies, and oregano. Zigzag 1–2 tbsp of mayonnaise across the cheese.

Bake & Broil

Bake at 200°C for 9–11 minutes until the crust is crisp. Switch to high broil/grill for 1–2 minutes until the cheese is blistered with golden spots and the mayo sizzles. Rest for 2 minutes before slicing.

Simultaneous Operations:

Slice onions, peppers, and protein while the oven is preheating to 200°C.

10. Easy Pizza
Ingredients
1 bare par-baked pizza crust (10–12 inches)

3–4 tbsp pizza sauce or prepared tomato sauce

Cheese: 1 to 1 ½ cups shredded cheese (mozzarella or cheddar mix, divided: ¼ cup for base, remainder for top)

Protein: 1 cup chopped Default Grilled Chicken or 2 cooked Seekh Kebabs (sliced into rounds)

Veggies: ¼ small onion (thinly sliced) and ¼ bell pepper (thinly sliced or diced)

Seasoning & Drizzle: ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ to 1 tsp crushed red chillies, pinch of dried oregano, 1–2 tbsp mayonnaise

Step-by-Step Instructions
Preheat Oven

Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Place a baking sheet inside to heat up for a crisp bottom crust.

Sauce & Base Foundation

Place bare crust on a tray. Spread 3–4 tbsp pizza sauce, leaving a ½-inch border. Scatter a light baseline of shredded cheese (~¼ cup) directly over the sauce.

Toppings & Cheese

Distribute chopped chicken or sliced kebabs evenly over the base. Scatter sliced onions and bell peppers. Blanket the toppings with the remaining shredded cheese.

Season & Drizzle

Dust the top with garlic powder, crushed chillies, and oregano. Zigzag 1–2 tbsp of mayonnaise across the cheese.

Bake & Broil

Slide onto the preheated baking sheet. Bake at 200°C for 8–10 minutes until crust is crisp. Switch to high broil/grill for 1–2 minutes until cheese is blistered with golden spots. Rest for 2 minutes before slicing.

Simultaneous Operations:

Slice onions, peppers, and protein while the oven and baking sheet preheat to 200°C.
